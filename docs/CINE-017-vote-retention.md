# CINE-017 vote retention and room purge

A recorded vote cannot be updated or individually deleted while its room exists. Deleting a participant, candidate, or round with votes is also rejected. The database permits a whole-room cascade only after the room is `EXPIRED` and its `expires_at` is at or before the database clock; malformed SQLite timestamps fail closed. This is a minimum retention guard, not a promise that deletion occurs at that instant. Foreign-key enforcement must remain enabled on SQLite connections. PostgreSQL's trigger checks the table's own schema under a definer with row security disabled; if that role cannot bypass enforced RLS, the operation fails rather than treating an invisible room as absent. Production roles must not be given rights to disable triggers or bypass these controls.

The existing expiry worker transitions eligible inactive rooms without deleting them and skips `MATCHED` / `EXHAUSTED`; no automated purge scheduler is added here. CINE-020 must define the scheduler, terminal-room retention period, authorization, auditing and race-safe expiry handling. Until then, an authorized database administrator can use a governed maintenance transaction: lock the selected room, verify the current retention decision and elapsed `expires_at`, transition an eligible terminal room to `EXPIRED` under that lock, then delete the whole room in the same transaction. An already expired, elapsed room may be deleted directly under the same approval and auditing process. This destroys all related room data and votes; no endpoint exposes it. Do not purge active or unexpired rooms. CINE-028 must review and deploy the migration, verify role ownership, RLS and rollback, and decide the production schedule and operating procedure before any production cleanup. This is an operator handoff, not an assertion that such approvals, jobs, or production migration exist.
